# Vision

## Purpose

SWI exists to make swarm intelligence understandable, inspectable, and useful
across disciplines. It connects biological observations, general mechanisms,
mathematical formalization, algorithms, robotics, and artificial-agent research
without flattening them into isolated encyclopedia entries.

The product is a living research system rather than a blog or news feed. Its
unit of knowledge is not merely a page; it is a sourced entity, claim, or
relationship that can be traversed, reviewed, corrected, and revisited.

## Long-term research direction

SWI follows this progression:

```text
Nature
  → collective behavior
  → swarm principles
  → formal mechanisms
  → algorithms and control
  → multi-agent and robotic systems
  → artificial-agent experiments
```

The defining long-term question is:

> Can a collection of individually weak, inexpensive, locally executable models
> exhibit capabilities that are substantially stronger than those of its
> individual members?

SWI does not assume the answer is yes. It will preserve this and related topics
as research questions until reproducible evidence supports narrower claims.

## Product commitments

SWI will:

- distinguish evidence, synthesis, hypotheses, and open questions;
- expose sources and review dates next to substantive claims;
- support Turkish and English without creating separate, drifting catalogs;
- connect natural mechanisms to their computational analogues;
- provide accessible alternatives to visual graphs and simulations;
- keep knowledge, graph, experiment, and simulation concerns separate;
- remain useful as a static public resource before a backend exists;
- evolve through small, coherent research slices rather than fabricated breadth.

SWI will not initially be:

- an automatically generated encyclopedia;
- an unsupervised research-ingestion pipeline;
- a leaderboard claiming a universal best swarm method;
- a 3D ecosystem or scientific-grade simulator;
- an LLM-agent demonstration presented as established science;
- a database-backed publishing system or account product.

## Relationship to the wider ecosystem

SWI participates in the `aserdargun.com` principle of one connected system. It
will align with shared public contracts for localized metadata, stable entity
identifiers, evidence counts, freshness, research cutoffs, and application
relationships. Generic infrastructure should be reused through explicit
contracts, not copied into SWI-specific variants.

The root portfolio should only reference SWI after the application has a real
public artifact and verified release identity.
